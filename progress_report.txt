MONTHLY PROGRESS REPORT - MONTH 5

Scheme: Provision of 11 kV Feeder, Distribution Transformers and LT Network
for Rehman Colony & Adjoining Areas.
Contractor: M/s Bright Line Electrical Works (Pvt.) Ltd.
Reporting Period: Month 5 of 8.

1. HT Line (11 kV Feeder):
   Pole erection is complete for the full 6.2 km alignment. Conductor
   stringing is complete for 5.4 km; the remaining 0.8 km near RD 5+200
   to RD 6+000 is held up because two private landowners have not yet
   given right-of-way clearance for pole foundations. Feeder has NOT
   been energized pending completion of stringing and earthing tests.

2. Distribution Transformers:
   4 of 5 x 200 kVA transformers installed and earthing-tested (values
   3.2, 3.8, 4.1, and 4.6 ohms - all within the 5 ohm limit). The 5th
   200 kVA transformer (near Block-G) is on site but installation is
   pending crane availability, expected next week. All 3 x 100 kVA
   transformers installed; earthing test results awaited from the
   contractor's testing lab.

3. LT Network (Aerial Bunched Cable):
   Stringing complete in Blocks A, B, C, D (approx. 3.9 km of the 9.4 km
   scope). Work in Blocks E-K has not started because the transformers
   feeding those blocks are not yet energized. Contractor has requested
   permission to begin LT pole erection (without conductor) in parallel
   to save time once HT energization is complete.

4. Feeder Pillars / Distribution Boards:
   5 of 8 installed. Remaining 3 are held at the contractor's store
   pending LT network progress in the corresponding blocks.

5. Meter Installation:
   Not yet started. Contractor states meters cannot be installed until
   LT network in each block is live. Revenue wing has completed consumer
   verification for Blocks A-D (approx. 640 of 1,850 consumers).

6. Site Issues Noted:
   - Right-of-way dispute for 0.8 km HT stretch remains unresolved;
     contractor requests SDO's assistance in a joint visit with
     landowners.
   - One 100 kVA transformer earthing test is overdue by 12 days.
   - Contractor's site engineer flagged that 2 disc insulators supplied
     in the last lot appear to be a different make than the approved
     sample; awaiting lab test certificate.

7. Overall Physical Progress: 46% (against a planned cumulative
   progress of 62% for Month 5 as per approved PC-I schedule).
