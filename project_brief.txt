PROJECT BRIEF

Name of Scheme: Provision of 11 kV Feeder, Distribution Transformers and
LT Network for Rehman Colony & Adjoining Areas, Tehsil Jaranwala, District
Faisalabad.

Executing Agency: Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (FESCO), Construction
& Development Division, Jaranwala.

Contractor: M/s Bright Line Electrical Works (Pvt.) Ltd.

Approved Cost (PC-I): PKR 214.80 million.

Time Allowed: 8 months.

Date of Commencement: 03-02-2026.

Date of Completion (as per agreement): 02-10-2026.

Scope of Work:
1. Erection of 11 kV feeder, approx. 6.2 km, on RCC/PCC poles with ACSR
   Rabbit conductor.
2. Supply and installation of 5 x 200 kVA and 3 x 100 kVA pole-mounted
   distribution transformers with HG fuses and lightning arresters.
3. LT network (aerial bunched cable, 4-core, 25 sqmm and 50 sqmm) covering
   approx. 9.4 km across 11 residential blocks.
4. Installation of feeder pillars / distribution boards at each
   transformer location.
5. Individual meter installation (single-phase and three-phase) for
   approx. 1,850 consumers, coordinated with the Revenue wing.
6. Earthing of all transformers, poles at road crossings, and LT network
   neutral points as per FESCO earthing standard.
7. Removal and safe disposal of the existing low-tension temporary
   network currently serving the area.

Key Milestones:
- Pole erection and HT line stringing: Month 1-3.
- Transformer installation and energization of HT feeder: Month 4.
- LT network (ABC) stringing: Month 4-6.
- Meter installation and consumer connection: Month 6-7.
- Testing, commissioning, and handover: Month 8.

Reporting Requirement: Monthly progress report to Superintending Engineer
(Construction Circle), Faisalabad, with photographic record of energized
sections and copy to XEN (Construction Division), Jaranwala.

Special Conditions:
- All HT/LT clearances to be maintained as per WAPDA/NEPRA Grid Code and
  PEC (Pakistan Engineering Council) distribution standards.
- No energization of any section without prior test report of earthing
  resistance (target: less than 5 ohms at each transformer earth pit).
- Contractor to submit as-built drawings prior to final payment
  certificate.
